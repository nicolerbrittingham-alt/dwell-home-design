About

Hi, I'm Nicole

I started Dwell Home Design in January 2022 with a simple belief: your home should be a peaceful, beautiful place you love walking into.

I design for real life. That means rooms that work for busy families, finishes that hold up, and spaces that still feel personal. I handle the boards, the shopping, the deliveries and the styling, so you get to enjoy the fun part.

And I'm not afraid to pick up a paintbrush or a hammer. Every project is a whole-home, whole-heart effort, and I'm flexible about meeting your family where you are.
