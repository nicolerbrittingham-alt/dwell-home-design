The process

How it works

A clear, simple process so you always know what's next.

  1. Discovery call

    We talk through your space, your style, your budget and your timeline. Free, 30 minutes.

  2. Consultation & proposal

    I visit or video-walk your home, take measurements and photos, then send a proposal. A 50% deposit reserves your spot.

  3. Design boards

    In about a week, I present mood boards, layouts and product links for each room. You get revisions until it feels right.

  4. Ordering & delivery

    Once you approve, I order everything, track deliveries and handle returns or damage claims.

  5. Styling day

    I arrive to place, layer and style every piece. You walk into a finished room. Furniture should be assembled before this day.
