Services

Design services

Every project starts with a conversation about how you live, what you love and what you need your home to do. Whether it's your whole home or one tired room, I'll shape the plan around your family, your budget and your timeline.

01

Whole-Home Design

My favorite kind of project: making your whole house feel like one warm, connected home. We create a plan for every room, then tackle it all at once or in phases as your budget allows. You get the same design boards, ordering help and styling days, at a lower per-room rate.

Custom quote, with a lower per-room rate for 3+ rooms

02

Room Design Package

The full treatment for the rooms that matter most. For each room you get:

  • Initial consultation and measurements (in person or virtual)
  • Design boards with mood, layout and furniture plan
  • A feature wall concept: paint, wallpaper or wall treatment
  • A curated shopping list with links and alternatives
  • Ordering and delivery coordination, including returns and damage claims
  • A styling day to install and style everything

$1,500 for your first room, $1,200 for each additional room. Includes one revision round.

03

Remodel & Renovation Design

Planning a remodel? I help you choose finishes, fixtures, layouts and materials, then work alongside your contractor so the design comes together as planned. When construction wraps, I come back to furnish and style.

$75 per hour, with an estimate before we start

04

Virtual Design (E-Design)

Not local, or love a good DIY? Get design boards, a layout and a shopping list with links, plus video check-ins. You order and place; I guide you.

$350 per room

05

Statement Wall Design

Board and batten, grid paneling, shiplap or wallpaper: a wall designed for your room, with the paint color and a materials list. DIY it or hand it to your contractor.

$200 per wall

+

Add-on: Home Staging

Getting ready to sell? I'll help your home photograph beautifully and stand out online, using your own furniture plus my accessories.

Staging consultations from $175; staging days from $600
